Two models we're building now

AirCast is the lab's family of AI weather models. One sharpens what we can see; the other stretches how far ahead we can see it.

AirCast-SR Super-resolution

A foundation model that turns coarse 25 km weather fields into kilometer-scale detail using latent consistency diffusion.

Global models see a county as a handful of pixels. AirCast-SR learns how terrain, coastlines and convection shape the atmosphere and reconstructs the fine structure that flood, heat and wind impacts depend on, at a fraction of the cost of a high-resolution physics run.

  • Latent consistency diffusion for fast sampling
  • 25 km input → ~1 km output
  • Presented at NSF EPSCoR SuperCollider 2026
Read the preprint

AirCast-S2S Coupled Earth system

A global, data-driven forecasting system that couples atmosphere, ocean and land to predict stably from weather out to subseasonal lead times.

Forecast skill weeks ahead depends on fast atmospheric dynamics interacting with the slower memory of the ocean and land surface. Data-driven models trained only for short-range accuracy pile up errors as they feed on their own forecasts, and break down at subseasonal timescales.

AirCast-S2S treats the Earth system as one multivariate state: three-dimensional atmospheric fields and surface weather, together with ocean and land-surface fields that carry longer-timescale memory. The whole coupled state steps forward one day at a time, so the ocean and land evolve with the atmosphere instead of being held as fixed boundary conditions.

  • One coupled state. Atmosphere, ocean and land advance together with a one-day time step.
  • Trained on its own rollouts. Multi-step training optimizes the next day and the whole week, so the model learns from its evolving forecasts and error growth is constrained.
  • Evaluated end to end. Atmospheric, ocean and land variables from daily to multiweek leads, for skill, stability and physical consistency.
  • The question it answers. Does letting ocean and land evolve jointly improve atmospheric forecasts as initial-condition predictability fades?

$2,000,000

A microgrid that keeps the Disaster Science Operations Center on when the power goes out

WKU's first award under Kentucky's Resilient Microgrid Program, funded by the U.S. Department of Energy's State Energy Program through the Kentucky Office of Energy Policy. Solar power, battery storage, existing backup generators and advanced controls on the WKU Innovation Campus will carry the DSOC through outages and manage its energy every other day of the year.

AIR Lab's part is the intelligence: data and AI applications that connect weather, energy use and emergency operations, so the grid knows a storm is coming before the lines go down.

Where the work reaches

Eight lines of research feed the flagship models. During Hurricane Beryl, the lab's AI forecasts outperformed traditional physics-based guidance.

  • AI-augmented weather prediction

    Deep learning that pushes forecasts from 25 km grids toward street scale. Our WMO Research Demonstration Project delivered ultra-high-resolution AI forecasts for the Paris 2024 Olympics.

  • Climate and Earth system modeling

    Physics-inspired downscaling, causal discovery and aerosol–cloud work that contributed to the IPCC Sixth Assessment Report.

  • Energy resilience

    Weather-aware control for the DSOC microgrid, linking forecasts, load and emergency operations.

  • Energy and commodity forecasting

    AI forecasting of U.S. natural gas prices, electricity and power demand, markets that move with the weather.

  • Flood and wildfire digital twins

    Real-time simulations built with EM1 and Kentucky Emergency Management, so responders can play out a disaster before it happens.

  • Emergency management decision support

    AI early warning and risk mapping embedded in the workflows of the people who respond.

  • Remote sensing and satellite data

    AI gap-filling for satellite records, commercial satellite products and Earth2Studio pipelines on GPU clusters.

  • Urban climate, heat and health

    SOLWEIG-GPU for urban digital twins, rooftop heat mitigation and the EpiClim climate–health dataset.
